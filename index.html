MiliMap is a paid Android app for maps, marks and tracks. Prepare maps for your work, mark points, lines and areas, and keep photos, audio notes and movement records on your device.

Offline map use depends on each map source's terms. Track recording uses a foreground service. Read the privacy policy for local storage, permissions and third-party network requests.
